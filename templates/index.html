HOW IT WORKS

Smart answers backed
by real match data.

Everything on WicketStats is built on one foundation — every ball bowled in IPL history. Here's how that becomes useful for you.

"Predictions use history, not opinion"

WicketStats predicts winners using past head-to-head data—runs, wickets, toss, outcomes—so results are data-driven, not guesses.

"Player profiles track every ball"

Every run, wicket, and boundary is logged by season, showing a player's career as it actually happened.

"Comparisons use equal metrics"

Players are compared using the same stats—average, strike rate, economy, wickets, catches—no cherry-picking.

"What-if scenarios recalculate matches"

Real matches are recomputed ball-by-ball to show outcomes without a player or under changed conditions.

"AI reads your data, not the web"

WICKETS AI uses your match data and stats to give precise, context-specific answers.

"Fantasy picks favour form over fame"

Selections are based on recent performance vs the opponent, not reputation.
