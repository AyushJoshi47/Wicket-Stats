Comprehensive IPL data analytics

Team intelligence

How every team has performed across all seasons

Full historical view from 2008 to 2026

Pick any franchise and instantly see their entire journey — how many matches they won each season, how many runs they piled on, and how many wickets their bowlers claimed. Trophy-winning seasons are marked automatically so the peaks stand out at a glance.

  • Season-by-season wins, runs, and wickets mapped on a single chart
  • Head-to-head records between any two teams, including toss decisions and venue history
  • Title-winning seasons highlighted automatically so standout years are never missed
Player analysis

A complete career breakdown for every batter and bowler

Look up any player across their entire IPL career

Search any player and get a full picture of their career — how their runs or wickets have trended season by season, which year was their best, how consistent they've been, and where their game has weaknesses. Batters and bowlers each get their own dedicated deep-dive.

  • Batters: season-wise runs, strike rate, boundary counts, dismissal patterns, and consistency score
  • Bowlers: wickets, economy, bowling average, and best seasons on a dual-axis chart
  • Player headshots and an AI-written career summary generated from real match data
Predictive analytics

Turning historical data into forward-looking answers

Match outcomes and top scorer forecasts for any fixture

Beyond describing the past, the system uses years of match data to make informed calls about what's likely next — from match outcomes and expected scores to which players are best positioned to top the run charts in an upcoming season.

  • Match outcome predictions built from win rates, scoring strength, bowling firepower, and toss influence
  • Top scorer forecasts that weigh both long-term consistency and peak performance spikes
  • 2026 season data already integrated so predictions reflect the most current picture available
Player comparison

Put any two players side by side and see who comes out on top

Batting, bowling, and fielding — all compared at once

Choose any two players from IPL history and the system lays out every meaningful statistic side by side — from career runs and strike rates to wickets, economy, and even fielding contributions like catches and stumpings. No guesswork, just the numbers.

  • Full batting comparison: matches, runs, average, strike rate, hundreds, fifties, fours, sixes
  • Full bowling comparison: wickets, economy, average, strike rate, four and five-wicket hauls
  • Fielding stats included — catches, stumpings, and run-outs all count
Match prediction

Know who is likely to win before the toss even happens

Every factor that actually influences a result, combined

This isn't a guess — it's a verdict built from every dimension of the game that matters. Head-to-head record, average runs scored, powerplay dominance, death-over finishing ability, toss advantage, and bowling strength are all weighed together to give each team a win probability score.

  • Head-to-head win rate between the two teams across all seasons they've faced each other
  • Powerplay and death-over scoring averages factor in alongside overall batting and bowling strength
  • Toss decisions, venue win records, and highest and lowest scores in this fixture all feed into the final call
